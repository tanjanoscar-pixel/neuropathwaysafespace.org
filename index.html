"We weren't asking the system for anything extraordinary. We were asking it to see her the way we saw her."

Like a lot of parents, I didn't set out to become an advocate, a researcher, or a builder of tools. I set out to get my daughter the support she needed — a diagnosis, a plan, a school that understood her, a professional who would return a phone call. Along the way, I learned how much of that depends on parents doing the system's job for it: chasing paperwork, repeating the same story to five different services, holding evidence together that no single agency ever sees in full.
