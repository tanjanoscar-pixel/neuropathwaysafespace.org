About

NeuroPathway: prevention is the cure

A digital early-identification and lifelong support ecosystem for neurodivergent children, young people, and adults — built from lived experience and professional insight.

What NeuroPathway does

NeuroPathway turns everyday behaviours, patterns, and wellbeing indicators into structured insight that supports:

  • Early identification of neurodivergent traits
  • Faster, clearer EHCP evidence gathering
  • Improved multi-agency collaboration
  • Reduced exclusions and crisis escalation
  • Lifelong support tracking from childhood to adulthood

It is needs-first, not labels-first.

A 2-in-1 ecosystem

Professional Support Tool

For schools, professionals, and services:

  • Structured observation tracking
  • Risk colour-coding (Low / Medium / High)
  • EHCP summary generation
  • Printable, PDF-ready reports
  • Data export (JSON)
  • Multi-role access (Teacher / Parent / Professional / Admin)

"Your Safe Space" App

For children and young people (8–17):

  • Emotional check-ins
  • Wellbeing tracking
  • Safe journaling
  • Pattern recognition
  • Voice-led, supportive design

Together, they create one continuous, connected pathway.
